About

I am a PhD researcher in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. Walter Zhe Wang. My research focuses on AI-driven energy system optimization and control for large-scale cooling and building energy systems.

My work spans the full chain from prediction and performance modeling to optimization, control, and field deployment. I work on load forecasting, model predictive control, dynamic programming, deep reinforcement learning, and large language models, together with the engineering required for BMS/SCADA integration, operational constraints, field testing, and continuous model adaptation.

I am also the Founder and CEO of Smart Cool Tech Limited, an HKUST-affiliated startup translating research on cooling plant optimization into deployable engineering solutions for commercial buildings.

Field research · First author

Field Demonstration of MPC for Chiller Sequencing

Developed and field-tested a data-driven model predictive control framework for chiller sequencing in a large commercial building in Shanghai. The deployed controller achieved 11.52% measured energy savings while satisfying operational constraints.

MPC · Load Forecasting · System Identification · Dynamic Programming · Field Deployment

AI for modeling · First author

AutoLFM — Multi-Agent LLM for Automated Load Forecasting

Developed a multi-agent LLM framework that automates key stages of building load forecasting, including data understanding, model configuration, code generation, and forecasting workflow validation.

LLM · Multi-Agent Systems · Load Forecasting · AutoML · Python

Optimization · Scalable deployment

Scalable Chiller Sequencing & Deployment

Combined dynamic programming for computationally efficient chiller sequencing with semantic modeling that separates control logic from heterogeneous building data, making advanced control easier to deploy across different systems.
